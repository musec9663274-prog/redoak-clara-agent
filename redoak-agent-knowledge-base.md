## Services
- Medical billing: charge entry, claim submission (837P), payment posting, ERA/EOB handling.
- Accounts receivable follow-up: aging AR review, payer follow-up calls, denial management and appeals.
- Eligibility verification: insurance eligibility and benefits checks before visits.
- Coding support: CPT / ICD-10 coding review with certified coders.
- Credentialing guidance: help practices understand payer enrollment, CAQH and PECOS (credentialing typically takes 90-120 days).
- Clearinghouse work: claims go through a clearinghouse (for example Availity, Change Healthcare, Waystar, Office Ally) before reaching the payer.

## Pricing (proposal — quote it, but a human confirms the final agreement)
- 6% of monthly net collections, $500 per month minimum, no setup fee, month-to-month.
- Alternative: $6 per claim.
- Promo (only on objection, and only for 2+ providers or $50,000+ monthly collections): 5% for the first 3 months, then 6%.

## How onboarding works
1. Short discovery call to understand the practice, specialty, providers, and current billing setup.
2. RedOak reviews recent claims and AR aging to find lost revenue.
3. Agreement signed, then the billing team takes over submissions and follow-up.
4. The practice gets regular reports on claims, payments, and AR.

## Common questions
- "Do you work with our specialty?" — RedOak works with many specialties; ask which specialty and note it for the human team.
- "Do you replace our biller?" — RedOak can take over billing fully or support an existing biller; ask what they use today.
- "Are you HIPAA compliant?" — Say RedOak follows HIPAA-compliant processes and a senior team member can share details on the callback.
- "Can I talk to a human?" — Yes: collect their name and best callback number and confirm a callback within one business day.
